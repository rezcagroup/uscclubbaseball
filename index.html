About the club

Baseball first. Brotherhood always.

Club Baseball at the University of South Carolina is the place for USC students to keep playing the game they love at a competitive level, while building friendships that last well beyond graduation. We're completely student-run, from coaching lineups to balancing the budget.

Off the field, the club is just as active: tailgates for home football games, a parents-weekend brewery outing, a fall mountain trip, an end-of-year semi-formal, and regular volunteer days in the Columbia community.
