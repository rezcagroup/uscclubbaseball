FAQ

Good to know

Any experience level?

Most players have a high school or travel-ball background. Bring your glove and come compete. Final rosters are set by tryout performance.

D1 or D2?

Everyone tries out together. Coaches place players on the D1 or D2 roster based on need and fit heading into the season.

What about dues?

Like most club sports, members pay dues to cover league fees, umpires, travel and gear, kept as low as possible thanks to fundraising and donations.
