Interested in playing?

Any USC student can try out

No roster spot is guaranteed, but every USC student who wants to keep playing baseball is welcome to come out. Fill out the interest form below and we'll follow up with tryout details.

How it works

Three steps to a roster spot

1. Fill out the interest form: tell us your grad year, position(s) and playing background.
2. Watch your inbox: we'll reach out with fall and spring tryout dates and locations.
3. Come compete: tryouts place players across the D1 and D2 rosters based on fit and need.

FAQ

Good to know

Any experience level?

Most players have a high school or travel-ball background. Bring your glove and come compete. Final rosters are set by tryout performance.

D1 or D2?

Everyone tries out together. Coaches place players on the D1 or D2 roster based on need and fit heading into the season.

What about dues?

Like most club sports, members pay dues to cover league fees, umpires, travel and gear, kept as low as possible thanks to fundraising and donations.
