About the club

Every dollar, every rep, every road trip. Student run.

The Club Baseball team at the University of South Carolina is the place for USC students looking to continue their baseball career at a highly competitive level, while making friendships that last a lifetime.

Two teams

D1 & D2

Both squads compete in the National Club Baseball Association, playing a full fall and spring schedule against regional club programs.

Flagship

Division 1

Competes in the South Atlantic – East Conference against Clemson, Georgia, Coastal Carolina and Georgia College.

Development

Division 2

Competes in the Dixie – East Conference against opponents like UNCW, Georgia Southern, Craven CC and Clemson (DII), giving more players the chance to compete and develop.

Club history

A first for the program

2025–26 Season

D2 makes it to Alton

The D2 squad qualified for the NCBA World Series in Alton, Illinois for the first time in club history, and came home with a 3rd-place finish.

More than baseball

The clubhouse doesn't close after the last out

As important as the baseball played on the field is, the off-field relationships we build with teammates matter just as much. Every year the club puts together tailgates for home football games, a parents-weekend brewery outing, a fall mountain weekend, and an end-of-year semi-formal.

The club also gives back: members regularly volunteer in the Columbia community, on top of the baseball season.
